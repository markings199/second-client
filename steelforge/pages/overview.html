What is ASTM?

The “American Society for Testing and Materials,” or ASTM International, is the current name for this organization. They create international voluntary consensus standards. To develop standards, tests, specifications, guides, and practices, committees of relevant industry specialists meet regularly, openly, and transparently.

DYK?

Modern steel sections are mostly recycled. The production of steel beams and columns has drastically changed over the years. In fact, those columns and beams are produced primarily from steel scrap, such as old cars and appliances.

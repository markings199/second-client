What is ASTM?
ASTM International publishes material standards used in steel design. The workbook export exel program EWIWIWI(S(STEEL SELECTION)).csv lists structural shapes and their properties (Type, AISC label, dimensions, and related values). Yield stress Fy and tensile stress Fu for each ASTM grade are defined in the material catalog in steel-db.js, in ksi, and are shown here and elsewhere in the hub.
